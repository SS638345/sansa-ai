About Sansa Voice

பேசுங்க, எழுத்தா மாறிடும்.

Sansa Voice turns speech into text. You speak, record or upload audio in Tamil, Tanglish, English and 15+ Indian languages, and you get text you can edit, copy, or download as subtitles. It is free, needs no sign up, and works in any modern browser on phone or computer.

Why we built it

Most voice typing tools are made for English first. Tamil speakers get missing words, wrong spellings, and English letters where Tamil should be. We wanted a simple tool that writes Tamil the way people actually speak it, for voice notes, call recordings, classes, interviews and video subtitles.

What it does

  • Live mic: words appear while you talk.
  • Record or upload: WhatsApp voice notes, call recordings, mp3, m4a and more, up to 25 MB.
  • Video subtitles: upload a video and download .srt or .vtt subtitles.
  • Output styles: Tamil as spoken, English translation, Tanglish or mixed.
  • Clean mode and My words: remove fillers and teach it names it should spell right.
  • My library: your conversions are saved only in your own browser.

Our own Tamil engine

Tamil is converted by the SANSA Tamil engine: an open Whisper model trained on Tamil speech by the IIT Madras SPRING Lab, running on our own server in India. Audio is converted in memory and not saved. Other languages run on your own device. See the Privacy Policy for details.
