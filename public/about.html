About SANSA AI

தமிழுக்கான AI குரல் கருவிகள்.

SANSA AI makes free AI voice tools for Tamil and English. Turn text into a natural voice, turn speech into text, speak in your own voice, change a recording to another voice, and clean up noisy audio, all in one place. You sign in with your Google account; every tool is free.

Why we built it

Most voice tools are made for English first. Tamil speakers get missing words, wrong spellings, and prices, dates and phone numbers read the wrong way. We build tools that speak and write Tamil the way people actually use it, for voice notes, videos, classes, shops and offices.

Our tools

  • Text to Speech: natural voices in Tamil (Kavya, Arjun) and English (Meera, Vikram). Prices (₹), dates, times and phone numbers are read correctly. Long text up to 10,000 characters becomes one MP3.
  • Speech to Text: live mic, record or upload in Tamil, English and 14 more Indian languages. Download as text, Word, PDF or .srt subtitles, or share on WhatsApp.
  • Voice Cloning: read a short Tamil passage, then type anything and hear it in your own voice. Your own voice only: we check that you read the passage.
  • Voice Changer: the same words in a SANSA voice or your own voice.
  • Audio Cleanup: removes background noise. It runs in your browser; the audio never leaves your device.
  • Voice agent: ask the SANSA agent about our tools by voice, in Tamil or English.

Coming soon: video dubbing, phone call agents and a developer API.

Built on open models, run on our own server

Tamil speech to text, the AI voices and voice cloning run on our own server in India. They use open models from research groups, including the Tamil Whisper model of the IIT Madras SPRING Lab, AI4Bharat Indic-TTS, OpenVoice and kNN-VC. Audio you send is converted in memory and not saved. Other languages and audio cleanup run on your own device.

Your data

We keep only what is needed: your Google sign-in (name, email, photo) and messages you send through the contact form. Your library and voice sample stay in your browser. Read the Privacy Policy and Security page for the details.

Who we are

SANSA AI is based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India.
