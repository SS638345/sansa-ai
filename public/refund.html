Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

The voice tools on sansaai.in are free. We do not take any payment on sansaai.in today, so there is nothing to refund here. The terms below apply to paid plans and credits bought on the SANSA AI app (app.sansaai.in), and to any paid plan we add to sansaai.in later.

1. What is sold

Digital services – subscription plans and AI credits – delivered online and usable immediately.

2. Cancelling a subscription

  • Cancel any time from your account's billing page or by writing to us. There is no cancellation fee.
  • Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for and does not renew after that.

3. Refunds for subscriptions

  • Full refund if you ask within 7 days of your first subscription payment and have used fewer than 50 credits.
  • After that, the current period is not refundable; you keep access until it ends.
  • Renewal payments are not refundable once the new period has started, except where the law requires.

4. Refunds for credit packs

A credit pack can be refunded only if none of its credits were used and you ask within 7 days of buying it.

5. Always refunded in full

  • You were charged twice for the same order.
  • You were charged but the plan or credits did not reach your account.
  • A confirmed fault on our side stopped you from using what you paid for and we could not fix it.

6. How to ask

Write to support@sansaai.in or the Contact page with the email you used, the payment or order ID, and the reason. We reply within 2 business days.

7. When you get the money

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Razorpay, usually within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank.

8. Chargebacks

Please contact us before raising a chargeback with your bank – most problems are solved quickly. An account may be paused while a chargeback is reviewed.
