Security & Data

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This page explains, in plain words, how sansaai.in is protected. It describes what we actually do today; we do not claim certifications we do not have.

Your data

  • Audio and text are not stored. Our server converts them in memory and returns the result.
  • Many things never reach us: other-language speech to text and Audio Cleanup run in your browser; your library and voice print live in your browser.
  • Sign-in with Google only. We never see or store a password. Your session cookie is HttpOnly and Secure, and we keep only a scrambled copy of it.
  • Own voice only. Voice cloning needs you to read a random passage that our speech engine checks, and the voice print is signed so it cannot be faked. The voice changer cannot copy another person's voice.

The website

  • HTTPS everywhere with HSTS; Cloudflare protects against attacks and floods.
  • A strict Content Security Policy only lets our own scripts run, and the site cannot be framed by other sites (clickjacking protection).
  • The voice engines refuse any request that is not signed in, and every tool has rate limits and size limits.

The server

  • The server is reached only through an encrypted Cloudflare tunnel; no ports are open to the internet or the local network.
  • The database and admin tools are not reachable from outside the server.
  • Each service runs in its own container with limited rights and memory.
  • Daily encrypted backups, with an encrypted copy stored off the server.
  • Health checks restart a stuck service automatically, and an outside monitor alerts us within about 10 minutes if the site goes down.

Report a problem

If you find a security issue, please tell us privately at support@sansaai.in or the Contact page and give us time to fix it before sharing it. Please do not test against other users' data or overload the service.
