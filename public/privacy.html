Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

SANSA AI ("we", "our", "us") operates sansaai.in and the Sansa Voice app. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

Google Sign-in (sansaai.in)

To use the voice tools on sansaai.in you sign in with your Google account. From Google we receive and keep only your name, email address, profile photo link and Google account ID, plus the time of your first and latest sign-in. We keep a sign-in session (a random code in a secure cookie) for up to 30 days, or until you log out. We do not get your Google password, contacts or files. To delete this data, write to us from the Contact page.

Account Information

When you create an account on the SANSA AI app (app.sansaai.in), we collect your name, email address, mobile number, and account preferences.

Usage Data

We automatically collect information about how you use our services, including pages visited, features used, AI generations, and performance metrics.

Content Data

We store content you create using our tools, including images, designs, documents, and AI-generated outputs, to provide and improve our services.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • To process transactions and manage subscriptions
  • To send important service updates and notifications
  • To personalize your experience and AI recommendations
  • To detect, prevent, and address security issues
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure servers with AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. We implement industry-standard security measures including access controls, audit logging, and regular security assessments.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service providers who help us operate our platform (payment processors, cloud providers)
  • AI model providers for processing your requests (with data minimization)
  • Law enforcement when required by applicable law

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Withdraw consent at any time

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. Analytics cookies help us understand usage patterns. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. After account deletion, data is permanently removed within 30 days, except where required by law.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sansa Voice (voice to text)

Sansa Voice converts speech to text. On sansaai.in, Tamil recordings and uploads are converted by the SANSA engine on our own server in India: the audio is sent in short pieces over an encrypted connection, converted in memory and not saved. Our web server keeps standard access logs (IP address, time, page) for security, not the audio or the text. Other languages are converted on your own device, inside your browser, unless you choose another engine; that speech model is downloaded once from Hugging Face and cached by your browser.

  • Live mic uses your browser's built-in speech service (for example Google in Chrome), which may process audio under that provider's privacy policy.
  • My library (saved texts, timings and recordings) is stored only in your browser on this device. Clearing site data deletes it. We cannot see or recover it.
  • If you choose Sarvam AI, the audio you convert is sent to Sarvam AI only for transcription, under their privacy policy. You can switch to the On-device engine at any time so no audio leaves your device.
  • Microphone access is requested only when you press a record or speak button, and you can revoke it in your browser settings.

Contact form

Messages sent through our contact form (name, email, category, subject and message) are stored on our server so we can reply, and are deleted when no longer needed.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via email or platform notification.

10. Contact Us

For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at support@sansaai.in or visit our Contact page.
