Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

In short: to use the voice tools you sign in with Google, and we keep only your name, email, photo link and Google ID. Audio and text you send to our tools are converted on our own server in India and are not saved. Your transcripts, settings, voice print and voice sample stay in your own browser. We do not sell data, show ads, or use your audio to train AI models.

This policy covers the website sansaai.in and its tools. It is written for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. SANSA AI, Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India decides how this data is used. The separate SANSA AI app at app.sansaai.in has its own sign-in and is not covered by this page.

2. Audio, text and voices you use in the tools

  • Tamil speech to text, text to speech, voice cloning, voice changer: the audio or text is sent over HTTPS to our own server in India, converted, and the result is sent back. We do not save the audio, the text or the result.
  • Other languages for speech to text, and Audio Cleanup: processed inside your own browser. The audio does not leave your device.
  • Voice cloning: you read a short passage; our server checks the words and sends back, signed, a voice print (a list of 256 numbers) and the speech part of your reading (up to 30 seconds). Both are stored only in your browser. When you make audio in your voice, your browser sends the reading with your text; our server uses it for that one request and does not save it. Delete both any time with “Delete my voice”.
  • Your library, transcripts and settings are stored in your browser (local storage). Clearing your browser data removes them; we cannot see or restore them.
  • We never use your audio, text or voice to train AI models.

3. Cookies and browser storage

  • sv_session – keeps you signed in (essential, 30 days, HttpOnly, Secure).
  • g_csrf_token – set by Google sign-in to protect the sign-in step (essential).
  • Browser storage for your theme, site language, tool settings, library, voice print and voice sample (stays on your device).
  • We use no advertising or tracking cookies. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views without cookies.

4. Services that help run the site

  • Cloudflare – network, security and website delivery; sees your IP address and requests while delivering pages.
  • Google – sign-in (Google Identity Services) and fonts (Google Fonts receives your IP address when your browser loads the fonts).
  • Hugging Face and jsDelivr – your browser downloads the on-device speech model and audio libraries from them.
  • Sarvam AI – only if you choose it and add your own Sarvam key; your audio then goes to Sarvam under their policy.

We do not sell or rent personal data. We share it only with the services above as needed to run the site, or when Indian law requires it.

5. Where your data is stored

Our database and voice engines run on our own server in Tamil Nadu, India. Cloudflare and Google may process data in other countries as part of their services.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to: see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete your account and data, or withdraw your consent (this means you can no longer sign in). Write to support@sansaai.in or the Contact page from the email you sign in with. We reply within 7 days and complete deletion within 30 days.

7. Children

sansaai.in is meant for people aged 18 or older. A person under 18 may use it only with a parent's or guardian's permission and supervision.

8. Security

How we protect the site and your data is described on the Security page. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects your data, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.
